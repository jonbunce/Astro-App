ABOUT THE APP
Allstars is the members' app for Wombourne Allstars FC, a volunteer-run grassroots football club in Wombourne, Staffordshire, England (FA Accredited, about 400 players from under-7s to adults). It is for the club's parents, players, coaches and committee, and is only of use to club members. The club is an unincorporated members' club, so the app is published by Jon Bunce, the club's website volunteer, on the club's behalf. The attached letter from the committee authorises the app and its use of the club's name and badge.

This is a parent account with one fictitious child in a hidden test team. It has no payments set up, so nothing can be charged. Accounts are the club's existing website accounts (the club's registration system is called ASTRO). "Create an account" and "Forgot password?" open the club website.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
- Home: next and recent first-team fixtures and the latest club news.
- News: club and team news from the club website.
- Family: each child's team, registration status, monthly subscriptions (membership fees) and payments, consents, and whether medical details are on file.
- Inbox: messages from the club and team news. Each is also sent by email; push notifications are optional.
- More: club information, safeguarding, contact, signed-in devices, sign out and Delete my account.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
More → Delete my account. After re-entering the password, the account is closed at once (sign-in disabled on the app and website, all sessions ended), and the club's registration officer and treasurer are emailed to cancel any Direct Debit and delete the member's records, keeping only what the FA and safeguarding rules require. Please don't use this on the demo account, or let us know if you do so we can restore it.

PAYMENTS (3.1.1 / 3.1.3(e))
The app has no in-app purchases and sells nothing digital. Members pay the club's monthly subscriptions for their children's football coaching, training and matches, which are real-world services delivered in person. These are paid by Direct Debit through GoCardless. The app shows what is owed and paid; to set up a Direct Debit or pay an outstanding amount, it opens the club's own website, already signed in.

SENSITIVE INFORMATION
The app shows a parent only their own children. It never shows medical details, only whether they are on file; medical details are entered and changed on the club website. Notification text is always generic (for example "New message from the club"). There is no advertising, analytics or tracking. The club's lottery (100 Club) is deliberately not in the app.

SIGN IN WITH APPLE (4.8)
Not offered: the app only uses the club's own accounts, with no third-party or social sign-in.
