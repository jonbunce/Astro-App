# Wombourne Allstars FC Privacy Notice

Wombourne Allstars FC ("the Club", "we", "us") takes your privacy very seriously. This notice explains
what personal data we collect, why we need it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what
your rights are.

The Club is a volunteer-run grassroots football club in Wombourne, Staffordshire, run by its
committee. We are the data controller, responsible for the personal data described in this notice.

**Contact us:** email contact@wombourneallstars.co.uk or use the contact form on our website. For
anything about a child's safety, contact our Club Welfare Officers at
safeguarding@wombourneallstars.co.uk.

## Who this notice covers

- players of all ages, in our youth, girls', adult and walking football teams, and children who
  come to taster sessions, Mini Stars or club events;
- parents, guardians and emergency contacts;
- coaches, team managers, volunteers and committee members;
- people who join our waiting list or the 100 Club, or buy from our online shop;
- sponsors, people who contact us, visitors to our website and users of the Allstars app.

## What personal data we hold

Personal data means any information about a person from which they can be identified. Depending on
how you are involved with the Club, we may hold the following.

**Your ASTRO account.** ASTRO is the Club's registration system on our website. For parents,
guardians and adult members it holds:

- your name, email address, phone number, the first line of your address and your postcode;
- your occupation category, which the FA asks accredited clubs to collect to help keep the game
  inclusive (you can choose "Decline to state");
- your employer or business, only if you choose to tell us;
- your sign-in details. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read them.

**Players:**

- name, date of birth, school, team or teams, and season;
- a head-and-shoulders photo, which we need to register players with the FA and our leagues;
- registration and payment status;
- whether you agree to photos and videos for club media, and whether your child is subject to
  family, care or legal proceedings that mean they must not be photographed.

**Emergency contacts:** name and phone number, and their occupation and employer if you give them.

**Health information:** health needs, medication, any medical training our coaches need, and access
and communication needs. This is special category data. We only hold it with the explicit consent of
the player or, for a child, their parent or guardian, so that we can keep them safe and look after
their wellbeing.

**Payments:**

- monthly subscriptions and what they are made up of, Direct Debit payment dates and amounts,
  whether each payment succeeded, and GoCardless reference numbers;
- Direct Debits are set up with GoCardless. We never see or store your bank account details.

**Waiting list:** if you register interest for a child, your name, relationship to the child, email
and phone number, and the child's name, date of birth, postcode, age group, football experience and
any disabilities or additional needs you choose to tell us about.

**Taster sessions, Mini Stars and events:** the child's name, the parent or guardian's name and
contact details, an emergency contact, and consents.

**Coaches, team managers, volunteers and committee members:** contact details, role and teams, and
the status of FA qualifications, safeguarding training and DBS (criminal record) checks, which are
carried out and recorded through the FA.

**100 Club:** your name, contact details, the numbers you hold and your payments. Members' names are
shown against their numbers on the 100 Club page of our website.

**Online shop:** your name, delivery address, email address, phone number and what you ordered. Card
payments are taken by WooPayments, which uses Stripe. We never see your full card details.

**Messages:** messages you send us through our contact forms or by email, and the club messages and
team news we send you.

**Website and app:** sign-in and security records, including your internet (IP) address. When you
give or change a consent in ASTRO, we record the date and the IP address it was given from. See also
"Cookies and website analytics" and "The Allstars app" below.

## Why we use your personal data

We only use personal data for the purpose it was given for. Mainly, we need it to run the football
club and arrange matches, to administer memberships and to provide the services you sign up for when
you register.

The table below sets out the ways we use personal data and the lawful basis for each under the UK
GDPR.

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Registering players and members, running teams, training and matches, and collecting subscriptions | Performance of a contract (your membership) |
| Registering players, coaches and teams with the FA (Whole Game System), Birmingham County FA and our leagues | Performance of a contract |
| Sending club messages, team news and match information by email and through the Allstars app | Performance of a contract. App notifications are only sent if you allow them on your phone. |
| Holding health information to keep players safe | Explicit consent. In an emergency, to protect someone's vital interests. |
| Safeguarding, including DBS checks and making sure children who must not be photographed are not | Legitimate interests in keeping children safe, and the safeguarding condition in the Data Protection Act 2018 |
| Photos and videos of players on our website and social media, and match reports that name players | Consent. For a child, the consent of a parent or guardian. You can withdraw it at any time. |
| Occupation category for the FA's inclusion figures | Consent: you can choose "Decline to state" |
| Your employer, so we can explore local sponsorship | Legitimate interests in finding sponsors to keep subscriptions down. Giving it is optional. |
| The waiting list | Consent |
| Committee members contacting members about club activities, renewals and social events | Legitimate interests in keeping in touch with our members and club community |
| Newsletters, promotions and offers from our sponsors | Consent, or as an existing member unless you have told us you don't want them |
| Sharing anonymised numbers with funding partners such as the local authority, or as evidence of local demand for pitches | Legitimate interests in running the Club and applying for funding |
| The 100 Club and online shop orders | Performance of a contract |
| Keeping the website and app secure, and understanding how the website is used | Legitimate interests in running a safe, working website |

If we need personal data to meet our responsibilities and you don't provide it, we may not be able
to register you or administer your membership.

## Who we share your personal data with

- **The FA, Birmingham County FA and our leagues**, to register players, coaches and teams and for
  affiliation. Coaches and volunteers are entered onto the FA's Whole Game System, and players may be
  too, depending on the league. Data on the Whole Game System is also covered by the FA's own privacy
  policy.
- **Coaches, team managers and committee members**, only what they need for their role. Coaches see
  the players in their own teams, including the emergency contacts and health information needed to
  keep them safe.
- **Referees, match organisers and facility providers**, when needed to run matches and events.
- **Anyone we must share it with by law**, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our
  players, members or others.

We also use these service providers, who may only use your personal data as we instruct:

- SiteGround hosts our website, ASTRO and the Allstars app's data;
- GoCardless collects our Direct Debits, for subscriptions, the 100 Club and walking football;
- WooPayments (Stripe) takes card payments in our online shop;
- Google provides Google Analytics on our website;
- Expo, Apple and Google deliver notifications to the Allstars app;
- Meta publishes the team news and photos we post on the Club's Facebook page. Photos only ever
  show children whose parents have agreed to photography;
- 1st Class Coaching runs our Mini Stars sessions with us.

We never sell your personal data.

## The Allstars app

The Allstars app is for parents and guardians, players, coaches and committee members. You sign in
with the same email address and password as your ASTRO account. Children do not have their own
accounts.

**What the app shows you.** The app shows information the Club already holds about you and your
family in ASTRO, and doesn't ask you for anything new. It shows:

- your name, email address and your role or roles at the Club;
- for each of your children registered this season: their name, team, registration status, monthly
  subscription and what it is made up of, recent and upcoming Direct Debit payments, any amount
  outstanding, their photography and legal consents, and whether medical and emergency details are on
  file. The app never shows the medical details themselves;
- club messages and team news sent to you, which are also emailed to you;
- club news, fixtures, results and club information, which are public on our website anyway.

To change any details, make a payment or set up a Direct Debit, the app opens the matching page of
our website, already signed in. Those pages work exactly as they do on the website.

**What the app collects:**

- **Your sign-in.** When you sign in, we record the phone's model name (for example "iPhone 16"),
  whether it is an iPhone or Android phone, the app version, and when the sign-in was created and
  last used. This lets you see and sign out your devices (More, then Signed-in devices) and lets us
  end a sign-in if your account is closed or your password changes.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, your phone gives the app a push notification
  address, which we store with your sign-in so we can tell you about new club messages and team news.
  You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.
- **Your inbox.** We keep a copy of each club message and team news alert sent to you through the
  app, and whether you have read it.
- **Failed sign-ins.** To stop people guessing passwords, failed sign-in attempts are counted against
  the email address and IP address used, for 15 minutes.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind. It does not access your
location, contacts, camera or photos.

**On your phone.** The app keeps only its sign-in key on your phone, in the phone's secure storage
(the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore). Your family's details are loaded from our website each time
and are not saved on the phone, and signing out clears them. On iPhone, website pages opened from the
app use a private browser session that ends when you return to the app.

**Notifications** are passed to your phone by Expo (650 Industries Inc., USA), which runs the service
the app uses to send them, and then by Apple or Google. We only send them your phone's push
notification address and a short, general message such as "New message from the club" or "New team
news for U9 Tigers". Names, medical details and payment details are never put in a notification; you
see the full message when you open the app.

## Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses cookies it needs to work, for example to keep you signed in to ASTRO and to remember
your shop basket. It also uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits and show us which
pages are used. Google Analytics doesn't tell us who you are. You can block these cookies in your
browser's settings or with Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The Allstars
app doesn't use Google Analytics.

## Data sent outside the UK

Some of our service providers are based in, or use servers in, the USA or Europe, including Google,
Meta, Expo and Stripe. Where your personal data is sent outside the UK, it is protected by the
safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or contract terms approved by the
Information Commissioner's Office.

## How we protect your personal data

We have put security measures in place to prevent personal data being lost, used or accessed
without permission, altered or disclosed:

- ASTRO only shows people what their role needs. Parents see their own family, coaches see their own
  teams, and medical details are only seen by those who need them.
- Our website and the app use encrypted connections, and passwords are stored scrambled.
- We never store bank or card details.

## How long we keep your personal data

- We keep personal data on players and members while they remain members or are otherwise involved
  with the Club. We delete it within 90 days of a player or member leaving, or sooner if you ask and
  we are able to.
- Taster session forms are deleted 90 days after the session, unless the child joins the Club.
- Waiting list details are kept until your child is offered a place, or until you ask us to remove
  them.
- App sign-ins end after 180 days without use, when you sign out, or when you sign out a device from
  the app. Ended sign-ins, and the notification address that went with them, are deleted within 30
  days. Records of notifications sent are deleted after 30 days. Your app inbox is deleted with your
  account.
- Some records must be kept for longer for legal, accounting, FA or safeguarding reasons, such as
  payment records and safeguarding concerns.
- Personal data on the FA's Whole Game System is kept under the FA's privacy policy, so we suggest
  you read that policy too. To have your data deleted from the Whole Game System, please contact the
  FA.

## Your rights

You may have the right at any time to ask for a copy of your personal data, and to have it
corrected or erased. You can also ask us to restrict or stop certain kinds of processing, including
direct marketing, and you have the right to data portability.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by changing it in ASTRO or telling a
committee member. Withdrawing consent for health information may mean a child can't take part.

A child who is old enough to understand their rights (usually from about 12) can make these requests
about their own personal data.

You can close your ASTRO account from the Allstars app (More, then Delete my account) or by contacting
us. Closing it signs you out everywhere straight away. The Club's registration officer and treasurer
then cancel any Direct Debit and delete your personal data as described above.

You don't have to share your personal data with the Club, but if you don't, we may not be able to
register you or administer your membership.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please contact us first. You also
have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection
regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time and will tell members about any important changes. If
you have any questions about it, please contact us at contact@wombourneallstars.co.uk.
