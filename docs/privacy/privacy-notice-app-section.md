## The Wombourne Allstars app

The Allstars app is for parents and guardians, players, coaches and committee members. You sign
in with the same email address and password as your ASTRO account on this website. Children do
not have their own accounts.

**What the app shows you**

The app shows information the Club already holds about you and your family through ASTRO. It does
not ask you for new personal information. It shows:

- your name, email address and your role(s) at the Club;
- for each of your children registered this season: their name, team, registration status, monthly
  subs and what they are made up of, recent and upcoming Direct Debit payments, any amount
  outstanding, their photography and legal consents, and *whether* medical and emergency details
  are on file. The app never shows the medical details themselves;
- club messages and team news sent to you, which are also emailed to you as before;
- club news, fixtures and results, which are public on this website anyway.

To change any details, make a payment or set up a Direct Debit, the app opens the matching page of
this website, already signed in. Those pages work exactly as they do on the website, including
payments through GoCardless.

**What the app collects**

- **Your sign-in.** When you sign in, we record the phone's model name (for example "iPhone 16"),
  whether it is an iPhone or Android phone, the app version, and when the sign-in was created and
  last used. This lets you see and sign out your devices (More → Signed-in devices) and lets us
  end a sign-in if your account is closed or your password changes.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the phone gives the app a push notification
  address, which we store with your sign-in so we can tell you about new club messages and team
  news. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.
- **Your inbox.** We keep a copy of each club message and team news alert sent to you through the
  app, and whether you have read it.
- **Failed sign-ins.** To stop people guessing passwords, failed sign-in attempts are counted
  against the email address and the internet (IP) address used, for 15 minutes.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind. It does not access your
location, contacts, camera or photos, and we never sell or share your information for marketing.

**On your phone**

The app keeps only its sign-in key on your phone, in the phone's secure storage (the iPhone
Keychain or Android Keystore). Your family's details are loaded from the Club's website each time
and are not saved on the phone; signing out clears them. On iPhone, website pages opened from the
app use a private browser session that ends when you return to the app.

**Who else is involved**

- **Notifications** are passed to your phone by Expo (650 Industries Inc., USA), which runs the
  service the app uses to send them, and then by Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android). We only send
  them your phone's push notification address and a short, general message such as "New message
  from the club" or "New team news for U9 Tigers". Names, medical details and payment details are
  never put in a notification; you see the full message when you open the app.
- The app and its information are hosted on the same servers as this website.

**Why we use this information**

We use it to give you access to your own membership information and club communications, which is
part of running your or your child's membership (performance of a contract). Notifications are
optional and only sent if you allow them on your phone.

**How long we keep it**

- A sign-in ends after 180 days without use, when you sign out, or when you sign out a device from
  the app. Ended sign-ins, and the notification address that went with them, are deleted within
  30 days.
- Records of notifications sent are deleted after 30 days.
- Your inbox is kept while you have an account and is deleted with it.

**Closing your account**

You can ask for your account to be closed from the app (More → Delete my account) as well as by
contacting us. Your account is closed straight away: you are signed out everywhere and can no
longer sign in to the app or this website. The Club's registration officer and treasurer then
cancel any Direct Debit and delete your information as described in "How long we hold your
personal data" above, keeping only what the FA, our leagues or safeguarding rules require us to
keep.
